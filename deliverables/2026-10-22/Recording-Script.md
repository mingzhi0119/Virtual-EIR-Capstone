## Copy-ready inputs

Goal: `Learn about evening students' dinner purchases through a small interview exercise.`

Success criterion: `Complete five interviews and record observations and limitations.`

Constraints: `Fictional classroom example. No spending or product build. Propose exactly one task with target_count 5: interview five students and record observations and limitations.`

Task report: `Two interviews completed. Both students mentioned price more than speed. Three interviews remain. This small convenience sample does not establish demand.`

Evidence observation: `Both interviewed students mentioned price more than speed.`

Source: `Fictional founder interview notes A and B.`

Limitation: `Two convenience interviews; founder-reported, not representative or independently verified.`

Criterion reasoning: `Only two of five interviews are complete. More observations are needed.`

Overall limits: `This is a learning exercise. The small sample does not establish market demand.`

Weekly results: `Two interviews completed; three remain.`

Weekly learning: `Price may matter more than speed, but the sample is too small for a conclusion.`

Weekly blocker: `Exams limit participant availability.`

Next priorities, one per line:

- `Recruit the remaining three students.`
- `Ask about their most recent dinner purchase.`

Use the actual reporting period and choose a next review date after its end. The date is displayed in the app; automatic reminders are not enabled.

Follow-up prompt:

> Review my latest saved weekly check-in. State the blocker and next priorities, planned/completed/remaining interview counts, and why this does not establish demand. Under 100 words. Do not change saved records.
