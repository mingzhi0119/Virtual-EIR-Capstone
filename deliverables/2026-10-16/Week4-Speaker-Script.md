## 3 — Persona: adaptation with common standards (30 seconds)

An engineer can receive technical explanations, an artist more experience-based language, a scientist hypotheses and methods, and a business professional business framing. The founder can override that starting style. Unknown or mixed backgrounds use a general style. These are communication preferences, not personality labels. ## 5 — From a goal to a draft (30 seconds)

For our fictional case, the goal is to learn whether evening graduate students face a recurring dinner-access problem. We enter success criteria and a limit of five interviews, with no spending or product build. The local model generates a structured draft through GOAT. We can edit it, reject it, or explicitly approve it. An invalid response must leave confirmed state unchanged.

中文提示：目标是学习问题，不是宣布市场验证；草稿须审批。

## 6 — Progress means activity (30 seconds)

After approval, the founder reports two of five interviews completed. The display shows forty percent of that interview task, with three remaining. This is not forty percent business validation. Two people form a small convenience sample. Repeating the same report should not add more interviews, stale edits should be rejected, and completing every task never automatically achieves the goal.

中文提示：2/5=40%任务；不是商业成功；小样本仍有限。

Fresh AI generation instead starts at zero, with a warning and history retained. We also have pause, resume and cancel controls.

中文提示：区分“保留报告修订”和“全新生成”；演示 v1/v2。

## Short Q&A

**Is Week 4 complete?**
The foundation is implemented and has recorded demonstrations. Final boundary fixes and regression acceptance remain open. Full R26 and the six future features are not complete.

**What is new versus reused?**
GOAT supplies the existing local inference API and appearance. Our EIR layer adds confirmed founder preferences, Persona selection, versioned workspace contracts, proposal/approval and task reporting.

**Does the model change saved facts or complete interviews?**
It proposes drafts. The founder confirms profiles, approves plans and reports progress. Interview execution remains real-world work; these examples are fictional.

**Does 40% mean the business is validated?**
No. It means two of five interviews reported for that task. Two convenience-sample observations do not establish market demand, and even five completed interviews would not automatically achieve the goal.

**Does revising a plan lose earlier progress?**
The report-preserving revision path carries reports into a new draft and keeps prior history; the recorded buttons reach v2 with 2/5 retained. Fresh AI generation starts at zero with a warning. Backend lineage and stale-proposal fixes are implemented; final workflow/edge reruns passed and foundation architecture acceptance was obtained.

**How do you avoid stereotypes?**
We use explicit preferences and a general fallback, with a policy against inferring personality from profession. The eight-response review still found founder/customer confusion, so the guard is an intention being evaluated, not a perfect guarantee.

**How strong is the evidence?**
It is a small fictional exploratory comparison plus deterministic and isolated UI checks. The pre-fix 65/10 snapshot is not final sign-off, a controlled improvement study, or Ain endorsement.

**Is it deployed online, and has CI passed?**
The dynamic application is local only. Vercel remains the older frozen recording. We make no current green-CI or production-readiness claim.

**Who owns the next features, and when are they due?**
Mingzhi is the implementation lead in the approved plan. Teammate assignments and deadlines remain unassigned until agreed; the roadmap windows are internal proposals, not confirmed faculty deadlines.
