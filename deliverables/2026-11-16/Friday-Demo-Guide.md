## Recovery and Q&A

**Slow or failed request:** let the displayed timeout finish. Preserve the draft. If the result is uncertain, use Recover saved state; no request is automatically resubmitted. An absent result may still be processing. A later retry requires an explicit risk confirmation. If the connection is unavailable, switch to the earlier recording or the warmed local fallback.

**Concurrent edit:** reload saved state, compare changes and deliberately resubmit. Do not overwrite someone else's newer record.

**Quality:** model advice remains fallible, including weak causal inference from observation and uneven style adaptation. Show the current review, not a claim of expert acceptance. A few fictional runs cannot establish real-founder usefulness.
