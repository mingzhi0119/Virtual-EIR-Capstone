## Three-stage integrated cases

The [complete workflow receipt](evidence/workflows.json) records three successful technical cases: Campus dinner at idea stage, Music practice at prototype stage and Window repair at growth stage. Each generates and confirms a plan plus experiment, decision, pitch and stage-review artifacts; saves contrary evidence and a weekly check-in; opens a fresh consultation; and exports a reviewed exact-count brief. The receipt contains no recorded failures or contract retries.

All three follow-ups include 5 planned / 2 completed / 3 remaining and sample size two. They distinguish users from payment approvers and leave the saved workspace unchanged. Read the remaining gaps alongside those successful state checks:

- **Campus dinner:** the follow-up moves toward institutional budget owners and vendor-payment documentation. The roles are examples and some language is conditional, but two reports do not establish that an institutional purchase pathway is the relevant route. Verify the buyer before adopting that test.
- **Music practice:** the follow-up says the founder assumes finding the budget owner is the only hurdle. That belief was not supplied. Pedagogical-value requirements are possible hypotheses, not known purchasing criteria. The proposed lesson-planning observation is only indirectly related to the actual payment pathway, and no participant count is specified.
- **Window repair:** the follow-up calls the two-person sample small/biased but omits the explicit “cannot generalize” statement. Vendor-compliance/documents are possibilities to verify. The response also contains an `e.s.` typo.
- **Decision Lab evidence limitation:** all three decision outputs are generic. The verifier supplies the same sample options and the assumption “Students can reach the service,” including Music practice and Window repair; the server correctly retains that founder-supplied assumption. This mismatch originates in the fixture, not a newly invented model fact. These cases establish contract/input preservation, not high-quality domain-specific decision analysis.
- **Experiment capacity limitation:** the verifier supplies experiment capacity five, and generation preserves it. This is not evidence that the model derived a feasible new capacity from three remaining interviews.
- **Pitch and readiness:** pitch feedback remains explicitly simulated and stage judgments provisional. Correct labeling and storage separation do not establish the substantive quality of every recommendation.

The deterministic mentor briefs remain the reference for saved state. Their compact selections disclose shortening and cannot replace reading full observations or obtaining human judgment.
